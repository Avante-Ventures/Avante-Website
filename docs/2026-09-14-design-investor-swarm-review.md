# Avante design and investor swarm review

September 14, 2026. Review of the current local website at `http://127.0.0.1:4315/en`.

**Recommendation: preserve the approved visual direction.** The real São Paulo journey, Avante arrival sculpture, distinct WIR and AlphaJuri exhibits, and identifiable team are the strongest parts of the experience. The next iteration should improve factual consistency, venture evidence, contact reliability, and small-screen reading.

Five agents reviewed the site independently: art direction, brand/editorial, UX/accessibility, an early-stage co-investor perspective, and an institutional LP perspective. A sixth agent challenged their findings. These are simulated professional perspectives, not feedback from actual investors or measured visitor research. No numerical ratings or conversion estimates are warranted.

| Perspective | What works | What deserves the next improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Art direction | The globe, real city and Avante sculpture create a coherent destination. WIR's expressive violet/cream composition and AlphaJuri's quiet porcelain/navy composition complement their respective brands. | Mobile content visibility, readable secondary type, more consistent portrait framing, and editorial continuity on inner pages. |
| Brand and editorial | The opening establishes place and purpose. The ventures have their own identities, actual websites and understandable propositions. | Consistent company relationships across pages; deeper venture stories; a clearer partnership action; complete Spanish paths. |
| UX and accessibility | Direct routes bypass the film. Mobile gets a static entrance and optional playback. Venture media is deferred and official static logo fallbacks exist. | Floating-control occlusion, focus management, small biography text, and exceptional WebGL recovery. |
| Early-stage co-investor | Named operators and two concrete businesses provide a reason to investigate. The Brazil focus distinguishes the studio. | What Avante specifically built, who uses it, the latest verified milestone, and the appropriate company-level partnership path. |
| Institutional LP | The presentation supports an introduction, and the people have attributable experience. The page keeps detailed investment terms private. | Precise attribution of historical results, consistent portfolio relationships, traceable benchmark sources, and a working investor contact action. |

**What to preserve.** Keep the optional cinematic entrance and immediate links to the companies. Keep WIR expressive and AlphaJuri orderly; forcing identical compositions would erase useful brand differences. Keep the official logo shapes and colors, including dots intrinsic to those marks. Keep the authentic team portraits and improve their treatment using the supplied photographs.

**Additional evidence work.** The benchmark citations exist, but `Footer.tsx:421` and `:423` link to organization homepages while naming specific reports. Locate and verify the exact primary documents and methodology before treating this provenance as complete. This review neither validates nor disproves the financial benchmarks. Industry-model benchmarks, team members' historical outcomes, and Avante's own results should remain clearly distinguished.

**Lower-priority refinements.** Harmonize portrait crop, eye line and color treatment. Inspect Portfolio and Library before standardizing their remaining rounded cards and dense introductions. Test the logo's lost-WebGL-context path: code can restore its ready state after a failed draw and hide the static fallback. Measure offscreen desktop hero initialization before deciding whether its cost merits optimization. Neither issue demonstrates that ordinary loading is currently slow.

**What the challenge round rejected.** No reviewer established that investors dislike the 3D or that the film blocks access. Both investor perspectives supported preserving the journey; the art director's hypothetical objection was not an actual disagreement. Screenshots of content behind the navbar warrant a settled foreground check, not a claim of a persistent transition failure. Small type is a readability judgment, not proof of a universal minimum-font accessibility violation. The six English process names are canonical, so they should not be automatically translated.

The useful difference between the investor perspectives concerns the next step: a co-investor wants company-level operating evidence and a relevant introduction; an LP wants precise historical attribution, exposure and reporting context. Both can be served without adding a second visual concept.

**Verification and scope.** The root reviewer inspected the live local site in native Safari, captured desktop and 390 CSS-pixel layouts, reproduced the failed investor button, and reread the critical source and canonical facts. The mobile captures use a same-origin width-review frame with unchanged app styling; they are not physical-device tests. Keyboard, screen-reader, context-loss and cold production performance tests remain outstanding. No financial data was independently audited. No application code was changed, no build was needed, and nothing was committed, pushed or deployed. The temporary review frame was removed and the existing local homepage left open.
