# Benchmark provenance check

Date: 2026-09-14. Bounded primary-source research for the local Avante improvements. No application or canonical-brand files changed.

## Result

The current ~50% versus ~19% pair is mandated by local brand truth, but the exact attribution and common 10-year framing were not corroborated. The existing “GSSN Annual Report 2025” label is unverified. A real GSSN paper and the exact Cambridge Q4 2025 report use different figures/context. This requires explicit factual reconciliation; replacing domain links with these PDFs alone would not resolve it.

The GSSN PDF identifies its own authorship and date; its hosting bucket is not the organization's domain. `https://www.gssn.co` failed to open through the browsing tool during this check. That failure does not prove the organization or a later report does not exist. Searches did not locate an official 2025 annual report supporting the displayed pair.

## Recommended citation treatment

1. Replace the unverified GSSN report title/year with the actual title and 2020 date **only when citing what this paper really says**. A neutral link label is “GSSN — Disrupting the Venture Landscape (2020).”
2. Label the Cambridge PDF precisely as “Cambridge Associates — US Venture Capital, Q4 2025; methodology and horizon returns.” Do not place it behind the ~19% statistic as though it verifies that value.
3. Remove unsupported shared-vintage or 10-year descriptors from the comparison pending reconciliation. Distinguish venture-company survey outcomes from institutional-fund net horizon returns; this review did not establish methodological comparability.
4. Do not silently substitute 53%/21.3% or 14.86% for the canonical pair. The discovered discrepancy should be recorded in the implementation report and reconciled with the owner of brand truth. Nor should an invented “illustrative” label be used to turn an unverified factual benchmark into an assumption.
5. If the current instruction is to preserve the pair, leave this specific provenance work explicitly unresolved. Other reviewed corrections can be completed independently. A public citation should not imply verification that the cited source does not provide.

No financial forecast or expected Avante return is established by these sources. Research used the primary documents themselves; third-party search results served only to locate the GSSN document and were not used as supporting evidence.
